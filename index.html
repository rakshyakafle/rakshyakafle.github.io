Pokhara

The Himalayan Country and the City of Lakes

Nepal is a small, beautiful country surrounded by mountains. It has Mount Everest and many of the tallest peaks in the world.

Pokhara is my favorite city in Nepal. It is famous for its calm lakes, mountain views, and fun adventure activities.

Key Highlights

Phewa Lake (Phewa Tal)
Phewa Lake is a calm, beautiful lake in Pokhara and the place where I always used to go with my friends and family. I loved sitting by the lakeside in the evening, watching the water, and taking a small wooden boat to the Tal Barahi temple in the middle of the lake.
Machhapuchhre
A famous mountain that looks like a fishtail, standing right above the city.
Sarangkot
A tall hill where people go early in the morning to watch the sunrise and fly paragliders.

Seasons

Autumn (Sept -Nov)
The weather is clear, you can see all the mountains, and we celebrate big festivals.
Spring (March-May)
Colorful flowers bloom on the hills, and it is the best time for hiking and trekking.

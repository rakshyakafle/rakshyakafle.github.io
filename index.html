Welcome!

I put this page together to show you why I love Pokhara so much. From lazy afternoons sitting by Phewa Lake to watching the sunrise hit Machhapuchhre, here is a quick look at my hometown pride.

  • Top places I love around the city
  • The best time of year to visit
  • My favorite local spots to eat

Top Places I Love

I love taking a boat ride on Phewa Lake, visiting the Peace Pagoda, and watching the sunrise over the mountains from Sarangkot

The Best Time of Year to Visit

Autumn (September to November) brings the crispest mountain air and clearest blue skies, while Spring (March to May) fills the hills with blooming rhododendrons.

My Favorite Local Spots to Eat

I enjoy eating Thakali thali sets by the lake, grabbing spicy momos with friends, and tasting fresh local fish.
